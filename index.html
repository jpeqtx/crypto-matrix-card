How to generate a password

  1. Choose a pattern (① to ④).
  2. Trace a path across the tiles — drag or click tiles one by one.
  3. Read the characters along your path in the chosen pattern's corner.
  4. Concatenate them to form the password.

Notes

  • Keep the seed and your traced paths secret.
  • The card alone is not a password.
  • Card size: 85.6 × 53.98 mm (ISO/IEC 7810 ID-1).
  • Generated with PBKDF2-SHA256 (200,000 iterations).
